About CineMatch

Less scrolling.
More movie nights.

CineMatch is a movie discovery and recommendation project built to make finding your next film feel effortless. It brings together an Indian cinema catalogue, a personalised discovery experience, and a recommendation engine designed to help you explore beyond your usual picks.

Whether you love Hindi blockbusters, Tamil thrillers, Telugu action, Malayalam storytelling, Marathi cinema, or hidden gems from across India, CineMatch helps you find something worth watching.
