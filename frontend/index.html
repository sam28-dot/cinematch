About the project

CineMatch.
Powered by data. Made for movie lovers.

CineMatch is a Big Data Analytics project exploring how movie metadata, ratings and genre information can support film discovery. This MVP combines live Indian and US-origin film catalogues, title search, movie details, related-title suggestions, and a browser-local watchlist.

The academic recommendation experiment uses MovieLens data to compare popularity ranking, TF-IDF genre similarity, and item-item collaborative filtering. Those experimental rankings are separate from TMDB’s live related-title suggestions; CineMatch does not claim to be a production-trained AI model. Live catalogue metadata and posters are supplied by TMDB.
